\begin{abstract}
Security context, typed statements on the assets, trust boundaries, weakness
types, requirements and controls of a change, is appended to the prompt of a
generator implementing a Java Highscore feature by generation and by reuse,
with functional feedback only. Researcher-written inserts lower failed issue
checks in all 18 context arms of two rounds without functional loss. Inserts
acquired by an agent from a repository snapshot and a one-line feature
description lower them further (Reuse 29 to 2, Generation 37 to 3 of 50)
but cost functional success (5 to 3 of 5). An audit of 124 final artifacts
traces the cost to one agent-written clause, skip recording when the player
name is unavailable; rendered without it, the effect stays (36 to 2 and 27
to 4 of 50) with 4 of 5 functional, and an acquisition rule that binds
rejection to the receiving operation and substitution to its caller gives
39 to 11 and 31 to 7 of 50 with 5 of 5 functional in both cells, replicated
by two further acquisitions per method. Limits:
one task, one repository pair, five trajectories per arm, descriptive
counts.
\end{abstract}
