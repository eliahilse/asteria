\paragraph{Functional.}
Under one response 40\% of the artifacts compile without security context
and 50 to 70\% with one; compiled artifacts pass 93 to 100\% of the tests
whatever the context. With repository tools and feedback every artifact
compiles and passes 96 to 100\% of the tests, with or without a document.

\paragraph{Secure.}
Compiled artifacts without security context pass 22\% (one response) and
33\% (agentic) of the ten security checks; with any of the three documents
82 to 87\% under one response and 86 to 90\% in agentic delivery, the
document written without any repository included. The judge leaves the
rate where it is (86 against 87\%) at 1.4 times the tokens. No
one-response artifact passes every test and every check; 0 to 20\% of the
agentic ones do.

\paragraph{Usage and time.}
One response reads 0.1 M input tokens in 1.2 to 1.9 minutes per artifact.
An agentic trajectory reads 0.8 M without and 1.0 M with a document in
about two minutes, 1.4 M in 2.7 minutes with the judge.
