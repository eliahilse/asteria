Security context for integrating a persistent ApoMario highscore feature, prioritizing the analogous ApoIcarus implementation. ApoIcarus records points, time, and a name at run-end UI actions and reloads the board; its generic implementation is singleton-based and network-oriented rather than the task's Path-backed API. ApoMario's score is held per player and is modified during level completion, while the level exposes an integer time and player list. The exact run-end lifecycle callback, player-name source, and whether ApoMario's time is elapsed milliseconds are not verified from the inspected excerpts and require careful integration.
