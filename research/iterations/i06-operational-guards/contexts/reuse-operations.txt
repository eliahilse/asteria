Corrected security context for integrating a persistent ApoMario highscore. The donor provides a highscore model with parallel points/time/name collections, a save/load mechanism, and descending-point display ordering, but its inspected implementation is network-oriented and parses unbounded line input. ApoMario exposes remaining level time and passed elapsed time separately; passed time is computed as configured time minus remaining time, while final score adjustments occur in ApoMarioLevel.setAnalysis. The exact target menu/panel source path was not readable from the snapshot, so menu lifecycle and the precise player-name source remain unverified.

[C1; recommendation; task; run completion recording] Record at most one highscore entry for each eligible completed run, on both win and loss, using final score, player name, and elapsed survival time in milliseconds.
Operation: Create and submit a run-end highscore record.
Untrusted input: Terminal gameplay state and repeated run-end notifications.
Invariant: Every eligible run gets one record only after final scoring; duplicate callbacks cannot add another record.
Enforcement point: At the terminal transition after final score adjustments and before leaving the run, guarded by a per-run recorded marker.
Failure behavior: Ignore duplicates; if recording or persistence fails, keep the game transition safe and report the storage failure.
Task relevance: The feature explicitly requires recording on clearing or losing with actual values.
Basis: prospective task requirement or reasoning; not an implemented safeguard.
Suggested verification: Exercise win and loss paths, including repeated completion callbacks, and verify exactly one entry with final values.

Operation: Derive and submit a run-end record from the completed ApoMarioLevel.
Untrusted input: Level state and player values at win/loss transitions.
Invariant: The record uses final authoritative player points and elapsed duration in milliseconds.
Enforcement point: Capture after final score adjustments and before changing away from the completion state; validate before modifying highscore state or writing the store.
Failure behavior: Reject or safely skip an incomplete or ambiguous completion rather than persisting fabricated values.
Task relevance: recordRunEnd(level) must read actual score and elapsed time from the level.
