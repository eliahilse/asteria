Add a local persistent highscore store, a finished-run recording path, and a menu panel. Reuse ApoIcarus’s parallel record model and descending-score presentation while replacing its remote service dependency with bounded local-file persistence.

Assets:
- Finished-run record containing actual score, player name, and survival time [integrity]
- Persistent leaderboard and its score ordering [integrity]
- Highscore menu panel and its displayed records [availability]

Trust boundaries:
- Level-end recording [entry point]: Player name and mutable level/player state at a cleared or lost level from The running game and local player-controlled or tampered game state to The operation that receives a new highscore record
- Leaderboard persistence [entry point]: Stored leaderboard bytes, including records and fields from The user-editable local store file to The highscore store’s parser and in-memory records
- Menu presentation: Records loaded from the local store from The highscore store to The dedicated menu highscore panel and its renderer

Task relevance: Protects the requirement that the board reflects the run’s actual non-negative score, player name, and elapsed time.
Basis: prospective task requirement or reasoning; not an implemented safeguard.

Task relevance: Protects persistence across runs and continued availability of the menu panel.
Basis: prospective task requirement or reasoning; not an implemented safeguard.
