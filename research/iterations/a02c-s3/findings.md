# A02c findings: Achievements under one response with the v13 generic document (S3), N = 5 per cell

Ten trajectories (Generation S, Reuse S+B), one response, strict delivery,
`gpt-5.6-luna` at reasoning effort high; the control is the no-context arm
of A02b. The compile and functional counts are in the [full hits](full-hits.md)
and the paper's second table; every security check of a non-compiling
artifact is unresolved. Across A02a to A02c the one-response protocol does
not produce a compiling Achievements artifact in these two cells with or
without a document, against 40 to 70 percent compiled for Highscore under
the same protocol: the second task's integration (points, kills, elapsed
time, run end, a new panel across five game classes) does not fit one
exact-edit response. N = 5 per cell; no significance claims.
