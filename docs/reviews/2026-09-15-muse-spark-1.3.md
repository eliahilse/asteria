- "plus a persistence round trip, in an isolated Java virtual machine with a 64 MiB heap and a 15 s limit" never says whether the heap and time limit apply per check or per artifact and never says how the round trip enters the failed slash unresolved slash passed counts.

- "When we write that an insert removes between 4.6 and 5.8 failed checks per artifact" presents an example range without naming the insert and cell it comes from, which is I20 Generation S where control range 39 to 40 minus insert range 11 to 16 gives total gaps 23 to 29 divided by five.

- "the harness kept 88 of the 90 statements produced and verified 342 of the 351 code locations they cite" never says whether 90 statements and 351 locations are totals over six documents and never explains what kept and verified mean for a listener who cannot see dropped statements.
- "The agent's full document as an insert, about 31,000 characters, gives 3 to 10 failed checks out of 50" never says whether 31000 characters is one document or both Generation and Reuse documents and never maps the 3 to 10 range onto the four I10 insert cells.
- "failed checks fall from 29 to 2 in Reuse and from 37 to 3 in Generation, between 4.6 and 7.2 fewer per artifact" merges two different controls into one range whose low end comes from I16 Reuse and whose high end comes from I16b Generation without saying so on first hearing.

- "changes nothing: 32 and 35 failed checks out of 50 against 33 and 35 for single-shot controls, at three to five times the tool calls" never labels which of 32 and 35 is Generation and which is Reuse and never tables the tool-call counts behind three to five times.

- "the insert arms need no more submissions than their controls (10 and 10; 11 and 13)" never labels which number is control versus insert and which pair is Generation versus Reuse, and one reading leaves an insert at 13 versus a control at 11.
- "2 or 3 artifacts out of 3 to 5 keep more than one hundred records where all controls bound them" lumps I20 with five trajectories and I21a slash I21b with three trajectories into one phrase, so a listener cannot reconstruct any single-cell rate.

## 2. Unexplained or invented terms.

- "the security context is the set of typed statements handed to the model before it writes" invents a term for what standard work would call a threat model plus security requirements, and the eight listed contents overlap without saying which are standard and which are new.
- "We call the whole conversation a trajectory and its final code the artifact" invents project terms without pointing to a standard equivalent such as attempt and submission, and "An artifact is functional when it passes all sixteen functional tests within the five submissions" redefines functional to mean passes this harness rather than the standard fit-for-use sense.
- "we call that agentic delivery, as opposed to single-shot" uses agentic and single-shot before a listener knows what searching the model may do counts as, and neither term is standard empirical terminology.
- "either in full or as a compact insert that keeps only the requirement and control statements" never defines full graph versus compact insert sizes or schemas on first hearing, while "Each statement carries a one-line failure behaviour" invents failure behaviour for what standards call handling or recovery.
- "Round I07 compares, in four cells, no security context against three kinds" and cell labels "Generation S" and "structural, functional or behavioural descriptions, marked S, F, B" never explain what S and F and B documents contain or what I07 and I10 and I21a name.
- "operations (what each step of the feature must do to stay safe), requirements (a constraint for each operation, with the operation named) and boundaries (where untrusted data enters and what to do there)" sound like three invented prompt styles rather than standard requirements versus design versus threat-model terms, and the later angles "data flow" and "catalogue" do not map cleanly onto them.
- "that range is the difference to the control under every way of counting the unresolved checks, from all-pass to all-fail; it is an identification bound, not a confidence interval" borrows identification bound without definition or citation, so a listener hears standard uncertainty language applied to a nonstandard worst-case range.
- "A sidecar watches an agentic trajectory and either injects the statements relevant to the code just read or edited" invents sidecar plus injects plus one-turn judge plus rewinds the conversation two turns without defining any of them as interventions.
- "the code at the run-end hook, the place where the game records a finished run" invents run-end hook without giving the method name or file, while "coupling tests that end a game run on the test driver's player" invents coupling tests and test driver for what a listener would call integration or fixture code.
- "compact, no failure lines" and "compact, protocol v9" and "v10, acquisition 2" in Table 3 are version labels that are never defined as instruction diffs, so a listener cannot tell what changed between v9 and v10 except through later narrative.
- "the operation that receives untrusted data rejects and never substitutes; the caller substitutes before calling it" uses operation and caller and boundary versus point where the feature does its work interchangeably without naming the store and hook methods at issue.
- "Artifact reviews and hook classifications are single-pass model judgements with cited lines, spot-checked by a second reader on 12 rows" leaves model reader versus second reader ambiguous between model and human and never defines row or rule or agreement.

## 4. Where the chain of reasoning breaks.

- "Step 1: does context help at all?" follows from the opening question, but its conclusion "Functionality is not traded away" is not supported because two Generation S arms and one Generation S+F+B arm fall from five to four functional while N equals five.
- "Step 2: can an agent write it?" follows from Step 1 only if researcher knowledge is the confound, yet its conclusion "So the agent's context is stronger than the researcher's and costs something the researcher's did not" compares different rounds with different controls and N equals five without a per-check or per-cell matched comparison.
- "Step 3: timing or force?" does not follow from Step 2 because the found cost is a specific hook collision and the next logical move is diagnosis rather than a delivery-timing pilot, and its conclusion "When context arrives matters more than how hard it is enforced" rests on three trajectories per arm with no table.
- "Step 4: where the cost comes from" follows from Step 2 but bypasses Step 3 entirely, and its conclusion "The cost is one sentence the agent wrote colliding with one property of the test fixture" moves from twelve test-only failures to a code-causation claim checked on only twelve rows by a model reader.
- "Step 5: removing the sentence by rendering" follows from Step 4, but its conclusion "Rendering cannot fix what the agent decided" generalizes from two cells where Generation recovers to four of five while Reuse still loses one run and an unnamed resource check worsens.
- "Version 9 adds one rule: reject at the boundary where untrusted data enters, substitute a default at the point where the feature does its work, never skip the feature's effect" follows from Step 5, but the lesson "The lesson is that the effect of every earlier insert was carried by the word reject at the store" leaps from one Generation collapse of 37 to 31 to a claim about every earlier insert without word-level evidence.
- "Version 10 therefore binds the roles: the operation that receives untrusted data rejects and never substitutes; the caller substitutes before calling it" is supported directionally by I20 and I21a slash I21b, but the closing "the result belongs to the instructions, not to one lucky document" overreaches because each arm still shares one document across three to five trajectories with only three v10 acquisitions.

## 5. Bloat.

- "We use standard terms" carries no content for a listener before a long definitional paragraph that could be replaced by naming the standards once when each term is first used.
- "which carries no knowledge of the repository" repeats the already stated repository-specific versus generic distinction in the sentence "Security context is not feedback from tests on generated code, and it is not a generic secure-coding instruction".
- "that line matters later" tells the listener to remember failure behaviour without saying why, which is teaser rather than information at that point.
- "Each round is declared and frozen before its first model call and completed regardless of direction; failed or degenerate acquisitions are kept and used, never regenerated" states preregistration procedure that a listener cannot verify and that repeats the fresh-control sentence about fair comparison.
- "Our replication is a second round with its own control that shows the same direction" repeats the reporting rule that every comparison is against that control rather than adding a new fact.
- "Table 3 follows the data-flow angle, which cites the most code" selects one of three angles without ever reporting the other angles, so the relative-citation justification is unused information.
- "So the agent's context is stronger than the researcher's and costs something the researcher's did not" restates the two preceding sentences about failed-check drops and three-versus-five functionality without adding numbers.
- "Injecting on demand changes when statements arrive, not how many, because the feature touches the very methods they cite" offers an unmeasured mechanism story that the pilot does not need for its timing comparison.
- "Rendering cannot fix what the agent decided" restates the I17 slash I18 mixed outcome as a slogan rather than reporting which sentence remained and where.
- "Delivering the context before the first attempt beats policing the attempts afterwards" repeats the Step 3 pilot conclusion verbatim in the closing synthesis without new evidence.
- "Counts are descriptive" adds no actionable meaning after the explicit statement that no significance claims are made with three to five artifacts per arm.

## 6. Scientific accuracy and honesty.

- "The model receives the task, the relevant source files and, in a context arm, the security context appended after the task" never names the model or settings or prompts, so the work cannot be reproduced or judged for leakage between agent and generator sharing one model.
- "With three to five artifacts per arm we make no significance claims" sits beside causal verbs like removes and beats and belongs to the instructions that invite a significance reading from ranges without variance.
- "Every round contains a fresh control arm without security context, collected at the same time with the same model and settings" controls within-round drift but the controls themselves swing from 27 to 39 failed across rounds, showing instability that total counts hide.
- "When we write that an insert removes between 4.6 and 5.8 failed checks per artifact, that range is the difference to the control under every way of counting the unresolved checks" is applied inconsistently because Step 1 and I09 report single raw differences such as 23 and 13 without bounds.
- "Functionality is not traded away: the control arms have 4 or 5 functional artifacts out of 5, the context arms likewise" understates the paired drops from five to four and treats passing a fixture that rewards substitution as equivalent to security rejection.
- "An artifact passes both only if the code at the run-end hook, the place where the game records a finished run, substitutes a name before calling the store" reveals the functionality cost as a collision with two fixture tests where the player name is null because it has no AI, not a real capability loss, yet the abstract still frames it as costs functionality.
- "A second, independent model reader checked 12 classified rows and the rule was refined until both agreed" uses a model to validate model judgements on fewer than ten percent of 124 artifacts without a human gold standard or agreement metric.
- "The agent's full document as an insert, about 31,000 characters, gives 3 to 10 failed checks out of 50, the fewest so far" overstates agent strength and omits that compact inserts discard most of that document without an ablation of length versus content.
- "The lesson is that the effect of every earlier insert was carried by the word reject at the store" overstates a one-word mechanism without quoting the inserts or testing word removal, while the later record-limit regression shows omitted requirements also move counts.
- "under every version 10 Reuse insert, 2 or 3 artifacts out of 3 to 5 keep more than one hundred records where all controls bound them, and none of those inserts mentions a record limit" buries a new security regression as an aside without per-check counts or insert quotations.
- "One task, one pair of games, one model as agent and generator" understates that each arm shares one acquired document across trajectories, so trajectory N overstates independent evidence for instruction generality.
- "The large-record check is often unresolved (43 of 80 artifacts in round I07)" discloses harness fragility but without per-check data a listener cannot tell whether totals are driven by untestable formats rather than secure code.

- "to minimize the knowledge of the prompt about the specific things of the specific tasks. So like more generalized. - "To try to draft an initial of a paper, meaning at least as bullets for a paper that refer to the motivation, refer to why we selected what we selected, and also some preliminary results with consistent numbers" is partly answered because "We work inside a real code base, a family of games maintained by copying one game into the next, where flaws travel with the copies" motivates selection and Tables 2 to 5 give preliminary results, but denominators shift between 50 and 30 and submissions are untabled, so one sentence freezing N and reporting bounds uniformly would answer it.

## 8. Three highest-value fixes.

- Original "One task, one repository pair and five artifacts per arm limit what the counts can claim." should become "One Highscore task, one ApoMario slash ApoIcarus pair, one unnamed model, and three to five trajectories per arm sharing one acquired document per arm limit what the counts can claim.".
- Original "The price is functionality: 3 functional artifacts out of 5 in each cell instead of 5, and 18 submissions instead of 7 in Generation." should become "The price is two fixture tests: 3 functional artifacts out of 5 in I16 Reuse S+B and I16b Generation S instead of 5, because twelve agent-insert artifacts skip recording when the test-driver name is null, with submission totals tabled per arm.".
- Original "What still fails are resource checks, and which ones varies by acquisition." should become "What still fails is tabled by check: per-check failed counts show which input, retention, parser, resource-stress, and deserialization checks remain, including the Reuse record-limit regression where version-10 inserts omit the limit.".
